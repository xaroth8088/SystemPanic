# Placed induction rail

Fire has deliberate placement edges. The first press plants a grounded terminal at the current player position. Release, move at least 48px, then press again to plant its partner. Holding that press energizes a fixed copper conductor between the newest and older pole; release after at least 0.22s sends one tangible armature from the newest pole toward the older one. Nothing tracks a target, orbits the player, encloses a captured area or spends a collected-ammo pool. A later press replaces the older terminal. Pressing within 48px of the newest pole recharges an existing pair without moving it; a lone pole cannot short itself into a shot.

Powered induction pulls both player and enemies toward the actual conductor from within 110px, with distance falloff and solid-cover occlusion. Actual conduit contact deals current damage; charge is 1.2s plus 0.12s per retained upgrade, capped at 2.4s. Release damage, impulse and armature width use that charge/upgrade count. The armature advances once through the host scheduler and may hit each enemy once. It stops at the old terminal, a real projectile-blocking shape or 2.8 active seconds. Full-radius substeps catch grazing cover for both the 4px current and wider armature; a round current cap cannot reach through a thin wall. Public Body.overlaps_circle queries preserve custom enemy contact footprints. Body-only rails pass current/armatures. Magnetic fields can modify public velocity, but mechanical rail guidance keeps the armature on its planted conductor.

Terminal/Conduit subclasses own their drawing and register through world.set_geometry. They do not block actors or shots. Level start and replacement clear owned geometry; unrelated swaps preserve poles, charge and held-input history while discarding host-cleared flight references. Rewiring cancels the prior armature. No fixed HUD meter or generic target marker. Local Upgrade captures art/chime, homes nearby and expires at 50 active seconds; successful collect_powerup gates its once-only sound.

Verified with tests/test_new_paper_ferric.py: 23 focused methods across Paper/Ferric, including an independently copied weapon with five foreign roles, thin/grazing cover filters, custom footprints, rewiring, unrelated-swap held edges, level cleanup and once-per-pass hits.
