# Rime finite stone rack

The weapon owns a finite rack of four physical curling stones. A throw consumes one carried stone. No time-based ammo regeneration exists: slowing down or aging never refunds a throw, and spent stones never expire. Below60px/s a stone settles, remains visibly on the rink indefinitely, and can only return to stock when the pilot's actual public footprint physically touches it. Newly launched stones must first leave the pilot footprint or physically bank/settle before pickup is enabled. A successful recovery removes that exact owned stone once, plays a quiet catch chime and emits a small ice-flake burst; no prose HUD or distant pickup magnet is added.

The first two collected upgrades each add one new carried stone, to a maximum rack of six. All later upgrades still strengthen damage and reduce throw cadence toward.3s from.65s. Damage29+6*upgrades retains speed scaling, up to four bank bonuses, real enemy impulse and.4s per-enemy rehit gating. Stock plus loaned stones stays equal to capacity during ordinary play. No timer, dead scheduled-object pruning or projectile age increments stock.

Flight uses <=4px real axis segments, independent blocks_projectile filters, actual shape overlap and a binary contact approach before rebound. Body-only rails pass shots. Every traversed segment checks the public pilot/enemy footprint, including custom shapes; it never substitutes a pre/post-bounce chord that might cut through a wall. Banks retain.86 of reflected velocity, impacts transfer impulse and reduce energy, and rolling drag is.22/s. A22px arena inset keeps the full rotating30px sprite visible and physically collectible at the rink edge. Moving/closing cover receives only bounded local collision separation through actual shape queries, never broad-box phantom relocation, ammo credit or a distant rescue.

Level start and gameplay-channel replacement reset the rack and retire the old scheduled stones cleanly. Music/background-only swaps restore the SAME owned stone objects after the host's scheduled-object clear, preserving stock, age, position and rest state. Weapon replacement retires its own stones. Unreachable foreign-pilot mixes get no extra movement, timer rescue or teleport restocking; awkward combinations are allowed by the product contract.
