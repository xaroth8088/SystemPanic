# Wind gates and jet corridors

The earlier broad ambient breeze is superseded. This role now owns physical wind gates and concentrated cross-arena traversal lanes. No host, pilot, enemy or weapon rule was added.

- Each Vane has a 36px hub plus two 14x74px solid wings. Its 208-square rect is conservative metadata; overlaps_circle/overlaps_segment query the three actual pieces. A damaging hit rotates both wings and jet by a quarter turn, with a .4s turn debounce. This closes the previously open lane and opens the perpendicular route. Rotation repairs newly intersecting actor positions through public geometry services.
- The blue 68px-wide jet is continuously active and extends from 26px ahead of the hub to the arena edge. It moves both player and enemies at 560px/s through normal collision resolution, with at most 80px/s crosswise centering. Actors can steer across the stream; riding its axis commits to rapid transport. No movement trait or sibling private state is queried.
- Hitting solid geometry or the arena boundary during a ride causes a local impact: player damage10+.3*level times local difficulty, enemy damage25+level. A per-body .9s gate prevents frame-rate damage. This uses the active actor's public hit policy. Player/enemy movement transports real held-thread endpoints without a special weapon exception.
- Two private-seeded gates are generated, three from level5. Build returns fresh geometry, consumes no combat RNG and clears old impact IDs. Update prunes IDs for bodies no longer present; deactivate clears the remainder. No body references or extra owned geometry are retained. navigation_key includes the actual center and discrete orientation, and shape bounds contain every wing.
